Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how JScan handles information when you use our website and tools.

● Last updated: November 15, 2026

Your privacy at a glance

JScan aims to collect only the information needed to provide and improve its services. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to JScan. This Privacy Policy explains how JScan ("we", "our" or "us") collects, uses, stores and protects information when you visit or use our website and services.

By using JScan, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please discontinue using the services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use JScan, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Your email address and authentication details when you register or log in.
  • Contact information: Your name, email address, subject and message when you submit our contact form.
  • Activity information: Records of tool usage, actions, file names and activity timestamps where the relevant history feature is enabled.
  • Technical information: Certain technical information may be processed by our hosting provider or other services when you access the website.
  • Uploaded files: Files you select when using JScan tools may be processed to provide the requested function.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through JScan may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect account access.
  • Provide the tools and features you request.
  • Maintain activity history for registered users.
  • Respond to contact messages and support requests.
  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse.
  • Improve the performance and reliability of JScan.

4. Account Information and Authentication

JScan uses Supabase authentication services to support account registration, login and password recovery.

Your account information is handled through the authentication service. You should keep your login details confidential and avoid sharing your password with anyone.

You can contact us if you need assistance with your account or wish to request account deletion.

5. Uploaded Files and Documents

JScan provides tools for working with documents, PDFs, images and other supported file formats.

When you select a file, it may be processed in your browser or transmitted to a service required to perform the requested operation, depending on the particular tool.

Do not upload confidential, sensitive or personal documents unless you understand how the relevant tool processes them.

File processing, temporary storage and deletion practices may differ between tools. Please refer to the relevant tool information where available.

6. Activity History

Registered users may have access to an activity history feature that records information about their use of supported JScan tools.

Depending on the activity recorded, this may include the tool name, action performed, file name, status and date of activity.

Activity history is associated with the user's account and is subject to access restrictions designed to prevent other users from viewing it.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

JScan may use browser storage technologies to remember certain website preferences and functionality.

For example, your theme preference may be stored locally so the website can remember your preferred light or dark appearance.

Your browser settings allow you to manage or clear locally stored website information. Clearing this information may affect certain website preferences.

8. Third-Party Services

JScan may rely on third-party services to operate and provide its features.

These may include:

  • Supabase: For authentication and database services.
  • Cloudflare: For website hosting and delivery.
  • Other service providers: Where required to operate particular tools or website features.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

9. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate JScan, provide requested services, maintain security or comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not permit service providers to use information entrusted to them for purposes unrelated to the services they provide to us, subject to applicable agreements and law.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information handled through JScan against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no website or internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should take reasonable steps to protect their accounts and devices.

11. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the services, maintain account records, respond to support requests, meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the service involved.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Ask questions about how your information is used.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details below.

13. Children's Privacy

JScan is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the matter.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, features or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

We encourage users to review this page periodically.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact JScan.
