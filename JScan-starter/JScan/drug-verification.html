IMPORTANT INFORMATION

What you should know

JScan helps users access official medicine information. However, a database search or barcode scan alone cannot confirm that an individual medicine pack is genuine.

  • A NAFDAC registration record indicates that a product has an official registration record.
  • A registration number printed on a package does not independently prove that the particular pack is authentic.
  • Do not rely on a barcode or product photograph alone to determine whether a medicine is genuine.
  • If you suspect a medicine is counterfeit or unsafe, do not use it. Seek guidance from a pharmacist or contact NAFDAC.
